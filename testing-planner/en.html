What is included and what needs checking

Each cabinet price includes standard flat-pack assembly, fitting, alignment, adjustment and suitable fixing, subject to feasibility. No drawer or corner supplement is added.

The cabinet and appliance involve separate tasks. The oven tall-unit price does not include oven installation. Worktop cutting and fitting the sink or hob are also separate: do not count a cutout already made.

Laminate worktops only; stone, quartz, ceramic and other specialist materials require a separate quote. New electrical circuits, gas connections, plumbing alterations and structural work are not automatically included. Listed connections require existing, compatible, accessible provisions.
